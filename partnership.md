## 📌 What Is MoveUp?

MoveUp is a premium accountability platform built for people who are serious about achieving their goals. It operates on a simple but powerful principle:

**Commit financially → Complete your tasks → Submit proof → Win the reward pool.**

Students and learners join private *accountability rooms* around specific challenges (e.g., *"30 Days of Coding"*, *"Learn Python in 21 Days"*, *"Ship a Project Before the Deadline"*). Each participant pays a real commitment fee to join. The first person to complete 100% of their tasks and upload proof wins the entire pooled reward. Those who fail to complete their tasks lose their commitment fee.

This creates a self-sustaining cycle of motivation: **real consequences + real rewards + peer accountability**.

## 🚀 Full Feature Breakdown

Here is every feature your students and academy will get access to through the MoveUp platform:

---

### 1. 🏠 Accountability Rooms

**What it is:** Private challenge rooms where groups of students commit to a shared goal for a defined period.

**How it works for your academy:**
- Create a room named after your cohort, course, or sprint (e.g., *"Cohort 12 — Backend Sprint"*)
- Set the challenge duration (e.g., 7 days, 14 days, 30 days)
- Set a commitment fee amount that every participant must pay to join
- Share the unique invite code with your students
- **Partners create and join rooms without paying any commitment fee** — your academy monitors without financial exposure

**Benefits:**
- Students have skin in the game — they paid to be here
- Creates a shared sense of urgency and camaraderie
- Rooms are self-organizing; no admin overhead for your team
- Multiple rooms can run simultaneously for different cohorts or tracks

---

### 2. ✅ Task Management System

**What it is:** A personal, private task list each student creates and manages inside their room.

**How it works for your academy:**
- Students create tasks mapped to your curriculum (e.g., *"Complete Module 3 exercises"*, *"Build and deploy a REST API"*)
- Tasks remain hidden from other participants until completed — keeping competition fair
- Supports **one-time tasks** and **recurring daily tasks** (ideal for daily practice goals)
- Each task has a clear pending/completed status with a visual progress indicator

**Benefits:**
- Students internalize the curriculum by setting their own task benchmarks
- Recurring tasks encourage daily discipline — perfect for coding streaks or reading habits
- Task list is a natural study planner that keeps students on track

---

### 3. 📸 Proof of Work Verification System

**What it is:** A transparent evidence submission system where students must upload proof that they completed each task.

**How it works for your academy:**
- Once a task is complete, the student marks it as done and uploads proof
- Accepted proof formats: **images**, **links**, **text descriptions**
- Proof becomes visible to all members of the room after submission
- No "I did it" without evidence — the platform enforces accountability
- **Partners and staff can view all task proofs submitted in every room they monitor**

**Benefits:**
- Forces students to actually demonstrate learning, not just claim it
- Creates a live portfolio of student work within the room
- Instructors and mentors can review proof in real time
- Encourages students to document and share their progress (builds a habit of visibility)

---

### 4. 🏆 Leaderboard & Competition

**What it is:** A real-time leaderboard showing how each member ranks within the room based on task completion.

**How it works for your academy:**
- Rankings are based on completion percentage and consistency
- Students can see exactly where they stand against peers at all times
- Cumulative progress is tracked — recurring task completions are reflected as continuous growth (e.g., 0/7, 3/7, 7/7 days completed)

**Benefits:**
- Healthy competition drives performance — nobody wants to be last
- Leaderboards create natural peer encouragement: top students inspire others
- Identifies high performers who may be ready for mentorship roles or advanced tracks
- Makes progress visible and tangible — students see growth happening

---

### 5. 💰 Integrated Wallet & Reward Pool

**What it is:** A built-in digital wallet powered by **Paystack**, allowing students to fund their accounts, pay commitment fees, and receive winnings — all within the platform.

**How it works for your academy:**
- Students fund their MoveUp wallet using Nigeria's most trusted payment gateway (Paystack)
- Commitment fees are automatically collected when a student joins a room
- All fees pool together into the room's reward pool
- Winners receive their winnings directly into their MoveUp wallet and can withdraw to their bank account at any time
- Your wallet balance is always visible on the dashboard
- **Partners and their staff are entirely fee-exempt** — they never pay commitment fees when creating or joining rooms

**Benefits:**
- Zero financial friction — students never need to chase payments or invoices
- Real money on the line = dramatically higher motivation and follow-through
- Transparent, verifiable pool that everyone can see — no trust issues
- Winners get rewarded for excellence, creating a culture of high performance

---

### 6. 📊 Personal Dashboard

**What it is:** A beautiful, real-time dashboard giving every student a bird's-eye view of their accountability journey.

**What students see at a glance:**
- **Active Rooms** — every challenge they're currently enrolled in
- **Tasks Completed** — cumulative proof submissions across all rooms
- **Pending Tasks** — what still needs to be done today
- **Wallet Balance** — current balance in real-time (in Naira)
- **Pool Size** — how much they stand to win in each active room
- **Days Remaining** — countdown to room deadline for urgency

**Benefits:**
- Students always know where they stand without having to ask anyone
- Reduces anxiety around "what do I do next?" — everything is laid out
- The dashboard becomes a daily ritual: log in, check progress, take action

---

### 7. 🔔 Automated Reminders & Notifications

**What it is:** A proactive notification system that reminds students to complete their tasks before deadlines.

**How it works for your academy:**
- The system automatically sends **motivational task reminders** every few hours for pending tasks
- Email notifications are sent when important room events occur (e.g., room reset, new member joins, challenge ending soon)
- Students are never caught off guard — the platform nudges them to stay on track
- **Partners can trigger manual room-wide notifications** ("Notify All") directly from their Partner Monitor dashboard, sending a custom push to every participant in a room

**Benefits:**
- Reduces dropout due to forgetfulness or distraction
- Keeps your cohort engaged even outside of live class hours
- Automated — zero effort from your instructors or admin team
- Partners have manual override to push urgent reminders when needed

### 8. 🔐 Secure Authentication & Privacy

**What it is:** A complete, secure user authentication system with password recovery and session management.

**Features:**
- Email & password signup with Supabase-powered auth
- Secure password reset via email

**Benefits:**
- Student data is safe and private

### 10. 🏢 Partner Management System *(New — Partner Exclusive)*

**What it is:** A dedicated role-based partner portal that gives your academy its own management layer inside MoveUp — entirely separate from the student experience.

**How it works:**
1. MoveUp generates a unique partnership code for your academy (format: `MUP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) with a configurable validity period
2. Your designated admin enters the code + your business name on the "Activate Partnership" page
3. Your account is instantly upgraded to **Partner** status
4. From that moment, the full Partner suite unlocks

**What you get as a Partner:**

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🆓 **Fee-Free Room Access** | Create and join any room without paying commitment fees |
| 👁️ **Observer Mode** | Join as a non-competing monitor — your presence doesn't affect the student pool or leaderboard |
| 👥 **Staff Management** | Invite your instructors and mentors as Staff members by email |
| 📊 **Partner Monitor Dashboard** | Real-time view of all your rooms' completion rates, member activity, and your staff's performance |
| 📢 **Notify All** | Send instant motivational push notifications to all participants in any room you monitor |
| 📁 **Proof Review** | View all task proofs submitted by students in your rooms |
| 📅 **Performance Filters** | Switch between daily, weekly, and monthly views to track student progress trends |

**Staff Sub-Roles:**
- Partners can invite any registered MoveUp user as a **Staff** member
- Staff get observer access — they can join and monitor rooms without paying
- Staff appear separately on the Partner Monitor, with their own performance tracking
- Partners can revoke staff access at any time

## 🎓 How the Partnership Works

Here's what a typical academy integration looks like:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1. Request Code** | Contact MoveUp to receive your unique `MUP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` partnership code |
