MOVEUP - PROJECT OVERVIEW

MoveUp is a premium, gamified accountability platform designed to help individuals and teams achieve their goals through financial and social commitment.

CORE CONCEPT:
The platform operates on a "Put your money where your mouth is" philosophy. Users join accountability rooms centered around specific habits or goals (e.g., "30 Days of Coding", "Early Morning Fitness"). To join, users commit a "fee" in the form of platform coins.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Join a Room: Browse active challenge rooms and commit the required amount of coins.
2. Daily Tasks: Complete the tasks assigned to that room before the deadline.
3. Proof of Work: Submit evidence of completion (text, links, or images).
4. Win the Pool: At the end of the challenge duration, users who successfully completed their tasks split the total pool of committed coins. Those who failed lose their commitment fee.

DESIGN IDENTITY:
MoveUp follows a "Modern Dark" aesthetic with heavy emphasis on glassmorphism, depth, and smooth motion.

- Brand Logo: Three overlapping circles arranged diagonally upwards, symbolizing a stairway to success and continuous growth.
- Primary Font: 'Inter' (Sans-serif) - chosen for its exceptional readability and modern, technical feel.
- Color Palette (Dark Mode):
    * Primary (Action): #6366f1 (Indigo/Violet) - Used for buttons, active states, and highlights.
    * Accent (Success): #10b981 (Emerald) - Used for task completion, rewards, and positive feedback.
    * Background: #0f1115 (Deep Midnight) - A custom near-black for reduced eye strain and high contrast.
    * Card/Glass: rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85) with 24px backdrop blur - Creates a sophisticated "frosted glass" layered effect.
    * Border: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) - Subtle outlines to define boundaries without visual noise.

KEY FEATURES:
- Dynamic Dashboard: Track active rooms, pending tasks, and coin balance in real-time.
- Leaderboards: See how you rank against other members in terms of consistency and coins won.
- Proof System: A robust verification system where members can see each other's proof of work.
- Real-time Notifications: Stay updated on room activity, member joins, and task completions.
- Motion UI: Powered by Framer Motion for liquid transitions, staggered list entries, and interactive hover states.

TECHNICAL STACK:
- Frontend: Next.js 16 (App Router)
- Styling: Tailwind CSS (v4)
- Animations: Framer Motion
- Backend/Auth: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Realtime, Storage)
- Deployment: Netlify

MoveUp turns self-improvement into a high-stakes, rewarding game, fostering a community of achievers who hold each other accountable.
